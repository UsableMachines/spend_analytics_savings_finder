What is this page?

The Dashboard provides a high-level overview of your procurement spend, supplier base, contract portfolio, and maverick purchasing activity.

Key metrics

  • Total Spend — aggregate of all recorded spend transactions.
  • Suppliers — number of active suppliers in your tenant.
  • Contracts — total contract count.
  • Maverick Spend — transactions flagged as off-contract or non-compliant.

Charts

Spend by Category and Top Suppliers show how your budget is distributed. Use the Analysis module to get AI-powered savings recommendations.
